> **Safety:** If you are locked out, use the **physical key** or the emergency power method first. Do not force the lock or the door. Keep a spare key with someone you trust.

## The lock does not respond

1. **Battery.** Replace with fresh batteries of the type the maker specifies, even if the app shows some charge. Weak batteries cause odd behaviour long before they are flat.
2. **Terminals.** Check the battery contacts are clean and the cover is seated.
3. **Phone connection.** For Bluetooth locks, check Bluetooth is on and you are within range. For Wi-Fi locks, check it shows online in the app.
4. **Restart.** Remove the batteries for 10 seconds and put them back.

## The motor struggles, jams or stops halfway

This is usually **door alignment**, not the lock.

- Test the door with the lock **removed from the latch**: does the bolt slide freely by hand?
- Check the **strike plate** lines up with the bolt and the door is not rubbing.
- Wood doors swell in damp weather (the monsoon). Adjust the strike plate or hinges.
- Re-run the lock's **calibration** in the app.
- Lubricate the latch with a **graphite-based or dry lubricant** recommended by the maker. Avoid oils that attract dust.

## Fingerprint or keypad issues

- Clean the sensor with a soft dry cloth. Wet or dirty fingers read poorly.
- Re-enrol the fingerprint with several angles of the same finger.
- Remove the old code and set a new one if the keypad is unreliable.
- Check the lock's **auto-lock** and **wrong-attempt lockout** timers. Too many wrong tries cause a temporary lockout.

## Battery drains too fast

- A weak Wi-Fi signal makes the radio work harder. Check the signal or add a bridge.
- Frequent **status checks** and heavy use consume power.
- Cold or hot temperatures reduce battery life.
- Use good-quality alkaline or the maker's recommended cells, not cheap carbon cells.

## The app says locked but the door is open

The lock reports the *bolt* position, not the door's. A **door sensor** (some locks have one built in) confirms the door is closed. Without it, the app cannot know.

## Locked out and the key fails

- Try the emergency power terminals if the lock has them.
- Call a **reputable locksmith**, and be ready to show proof that you live there.
- Do not drill the lock yourself.

## Secure the account

If the lock acted on its own or someone else may have had access, change the account password, turn on 2FA, review the user and code list, and check the access log. See [accounts and 2FA](/learn/accounts-passwords-and-2fa).

## When to escalate

If the lock still fails after new batteries, alignment checks and a reset, contact the manufacturer with the model, firmware and what you tried ([when to reset or ask for help](/learn/when-to-reset-or-ask-for-help)).
