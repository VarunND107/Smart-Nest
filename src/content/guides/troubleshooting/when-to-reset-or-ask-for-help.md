A factory reset is powerful and often the last useful step. Used too early it just creates extra work.

## What a reset does

A factory reset typically erases the device's Wi-Fi details, pairings, schedules and local settings, returning it to out-of-box state. It does **not** reliably remove the device from your cloud account, so remove it in the app first ([selling or gifting devices](/learn/selling-or-gifting-devices)).

## Before you reset

1. **Record the settings** you want to recreate: names, rooms, schedules, scenes, camera zones.
2. **Remove it** from the app and from other ecosystems.
3. **Note the firmware version** and the exact model.
4. For hubs: check whether the child devices will need to be re-paired.
5. For locks: make sure you have the physical key.

## When a reset is the right move

- The device does not respond to power-cycling and re-pairing.
- It is stuck in a bad state after a failed update.
- You are about to pass it on.
- It was previously set up on someone else's account.

## When it is not

- The **router or Wi-Fi** is the problem. A reset will not fix a weak signal.
- The **vendor's cloud** is down.
- The issue is in an **automation**, not the device ([automations not triggering](/learn/automations-not-triggering)).

## Ask for help effectively

A good support request gets answered the first time. Include:

1. **Model and firmware version.**
2. **What you expected** and **what happened**.
3. **When it started** and what changed just before it.
4. **What you already tried**, in order.
5. **Screenshots** of the error or the app screen.
6. **Your network:** router model, Wi-Fi band, number of devices.
7. **Purchase date and proof** of purchase if you want a warranty claim.

Avoid sending passwords in a support message.

## Warranty basics

Keep the **invoice** and note the warranty end date. If a device is faulty within the warranty or return window, claim it rather than repairing it yourself. Many problems need replacement, not another reset. The device record in SmartNest (My home & devices) is being built to hold these dates.

## About SmartNest Care

SmartNest Care is a support-ticket system being built into this project so you can raise a problem against a device and follow every update. It is a **demonstration** support desk for a student project. It does not connect you to real technicians or manufacturers, and it cannot access your devices. For real warranty or repair work, contact the manufacturer or your retailer.
