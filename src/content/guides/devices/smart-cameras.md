A smart camera streams video to your phone and can send alerts when it sees motion or a person. It is also the device most likely to affect your privacy, so choose and place it deliberately.

## Indoor, outdoor and form

- **Indoor** cameras are cheap and usually plug into a socket. Pan-and-tilt models cover a whole room.
- **Outdoor** cameras need a weather-rating (look for an IP rating) and a power plan. A mains cable run, a power adapter near the mount, or Power over Ethernet.
- **Battery** cameras are easy to place but need recharging and often record only short event clips.

## Wi-Fi, Ethernet or PoE

| Connection | Strength | Weakness |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wi-Fi | Easy install | Weak signal ruins video; uses router capacity |
| Ethernet + separate power | Stable | Cable runs |
| PoE (Power over Ethernet) | One cable for data and power | Needs a PoE switch or injector |

For outdoor positions far from the router, wired is far more reliable than hoping Wi-Fi reaches.

## Resolution and night vision

Higher resolution (2 MP, 3 MP, 4 MP and up) shows more detail but uses more bandwidth and storage. For a doorway, 2 to 3 MP is typically enough; wider areas benefit from more. Night vision uses infrared LEDs and usually renders in black and white. Glass in front of the camera reflects the IR light and washes out the picture, so mount cameras outside windows or use the camera's reflection-avoidance tips.

## Where recordings are stored

- **microSD card in the camera:** private and subscription-free, but lost if the camera is stolen.
- **Cloud:** off-site and convenient, usually a subscription, and your footage lives on someone else's servers.
- **NAS or network recorder:** you keep control; more setup.

Understand which one the product uses *before* you buy. Check what happens to old footage and when it is deleted.

## Privacy and legal basics

- Point cameras at **your own property**. Avoid capturing neighbours' windows or shared passages.
- Tell household members and visitors where cameras are, especially indoors.
- Audio recording can have extra consent requirements. Disable it if you do not need it.

## Security checklist

- Change the default password and turn on two-factor authentication where offered ([accounts and 2FA](/learn/accounts-passwords-and-2fa)).
- Check the vendor's update record ([firmware and end of support](/learn/firmware-updates-and-end-of-support)).
- Disable features you will not use, such as remote viewing without login or public sharing links.
