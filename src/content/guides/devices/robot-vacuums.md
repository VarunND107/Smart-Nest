A robot vacuum cleans on a schedule or on command and returns to its dock. It is a smart device that moves around your home, so navigation quality and data handling both matter.

## Navigation types

| Type | How it works | Strengths | Weaknesses |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Random/bump | Drives until it hits something | Cheapest | Misses areas, takes long |
| Gyroscope pattern | Follows rows using motion sensing | Better coverage | No map, no room selection |
| LiDAR | Spins a laser to map rooms | Accurate maps, room-by-room cleaning, works in the dark | Taller body can get stuck under low furniture |
| Camera (vSLAM or AI) | Uses a camera to recognise rooms and obstacles | Obstacle recognition | Camera inside your home, lighting-dependent |

## Things that matter in many Indian homes

- **Dust and fine sand** can clog filters quickly; check filter and brush maintenance costs.
- **Thresholds and rugs:** check the maximum climb height; floor transitions between rooms can stop a robot.
- **Loose cables, slippers and pet waste** are the usual causes of getting stuck. Tidy first.
- **Mopping modules** differ widely; a wet pad on a basic model mostly wipes a damp surface.
- **Spare parts and service:** confirm you can buy brushes, filters and batteries locally.

## Maps, cloud and privacy

Most vacuums create a **floor plan** of your home and store it in the maker's cloud so the app can show it. Camera-equipped models may also upload images for obstacle recognition.

- Read what the app says about map and image storage.
- Where offered, **turn off** cloud features you do not use, or disable the camera.
- Do not put the robot on a network segment you cannot control. See [router and network segmentation](/learn/router-and-network-segmentation).
- When selling the device, remove it from your account and factory reset it. See [selling or gifting devices](/learn/selling-or-gifting-devices).

## Keep it reliable

1. Empty the bin and clean brushes after every few runs.
2. Tidy cables and small objects before scheduled runs.
3. Set **no-go zones** around pet bowls and cables if the app offers them.
4. Schedule cleaning for when the house is empty ([routines for comfort](/learn/routines-for-comfort)).

If the robot loses connection often, see [device offline troubleshooting](/learn/device-offline-troubleshooting-flow).
