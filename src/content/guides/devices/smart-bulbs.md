A smart bulb is an LED lamp with a radio inside. You can dim it, change its colour temperature (and colour on some models), schedule it, and group it with others.

## Check the fitting first

Most Indian homes use **B22 bayonet** (two pins) fittings in ceiling and bracket lights, while **E27** screw bases are common in lamps and imported fittings, and **E14** small screw bases in decorative fixtures. Smart bulbs are sold in all three, but the choice in B22 is wider in some product lines than others. Always check before buying.

## White, tunable white or colour

| Type | Good for | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dimmable white | Bedrooms, corridors | Cheapest; still gets scheduling and remote control |
| Tunable white | Study, living room | Cool white for work, warm white in the evening |
| Colour | Mood, entertainment | Colours are less bright than white mode |

## The wall-switch problem

A smart bulb must keep receiving power to stay reachable. If someone flips the **wall switch off**, the bulb vanishes from the app and from voice control. This is the number one complaint.

Options:

- Leave the wall switch on and use the app, voice or a battery wireless button.
- Fit a [smart switch or relay](/learn/smart-switches-and-relays) *instead* and keep ordinary bulbs. This often suits Indian homes better because anyone can still use the wall switch.
- Use bulbs that support a "wall-switch-safe" feature where flicking the switch restores normal lighting.

## Wi-Fi or Zigbee

- **Wi-Fi bulbs** need no hub, but many bulbs on a basic router can cause drops.
- **Zigbee or Thread bulbs** use a hub or border router, respond faster in groups and keep Wi-Fi free. See [how devices connect](/learn/how-smart-devices-connect).

## Behaviour after a power cut

Check what the bulb does when power returns. Some come back on at full brightness, which is unpleasant at 3 a.m. Look for a configurable power-on behaviour.

## Setup tips

- Name bulbs by **room and position** ("Study ceiling"), not "Bulb 3". See [voice assistants, rooms and naming](/learn/voice-assistants-rooms-and-naming).
- Group bulbs in a room so one command changes them together.
- If a bulb will not pair, see [device won't pair](/learn/device-wont-pair).

Ideas for what to do with them are in [lighting automations](/learn/lighting-automations).
