A smart lock lets you unlock a door with a fingerprint, code, card, phone or key, and can keep a log of who opened it. It protects a door, so reliability matters more than features.

## Two kinds

- **Retrofit units** fit over your existing deadbolt on the inside and keep the outside key cylinder. They are easier to fit and reversible.
- **Replacement locks** swap out the whole lock body. They tend to be sturdier but depend on your door and mortise type.

Check your door type (wood, metal, glass), door thickness and lock type (deadbolt, mortise) against the product's specifications before buying.

## Ways to unlock

| Method | Good | Watch out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fingerprint | Fast for family | Wet or dirty hands can fail |
| PIN code | Easy to share and revoke | Keypad wear can reveal likely digits |
| RFID card/tag | Handy for staff or kids | Lost cards need to be removed |
| Phone app (Bluetooth/Wi-Fi) | Remote and temporary access | Needs phone battery and updates |
| Mechanical key | Always works | Keep a spare outside the house |

## Always keep a mechanical backup

Batteries die and electronics fail. Choose a lock that has a **physical key override** or a way to power it externally in an emergency. Keep a spare key with someone you trust, not under the mat. Do not buy a lock that offers *only* electronic access for your main door unless you accept that risk.

## Battery

Fit fresh batteries of the type the maker specifies and pay attention to low-battery alerts. Replace on the first warning rather than waiting.

## Sharing access safely

- Give family permanent codes. Give guests, cleaners and delivery staff **temporary or scheduled** codes.
- Remove access when it is no longer needed. Review the access list every few months.
- Do not hand out the master account; use separate users so the log shows who entered.

## Security points

- Turn on two-factor authentication for the lock's account ([accounts and 2FA](/learn/accounts-passwords-and-2fa)).
- Use a long PIN and avoid birthdays or repeating digits.
- Do not connect the lock to voice commands that can unlock without a PIN. A voice assistant overheard through a window should never open your door.
- Never automate unlocking based on a camera, a doorbell press or a schedule alone ([designing automations safely](/learn/designing-automations-safely)).
