Matter is an industry standard for smart-home devices, developed by a group of large manufacturers. Its goal is simple: a device that carries the Matter logo should work with any Matter-capable app or assistant, instead of each brand needing its own integration.

## What it improves

- **Interoperability.** A Matter bulb can be added to Google Home, Alexa, Apple Home or SmartThings.
- **Multi-admin.** One device can belong to several ecosystems at the same time, so family members using different phones can all control it.
- **Local control.** Matter devices talk to your controller over your home network, so basic control keeps working when the internet is down.
- **Simpler pairing.** Setup usually uses a QR code or numeric code, with Bluetooth Low Energy helping the first connection.

## How it runs

Matter runs over **Wi-Fi**, **Ethernet** and **Thread** (a low-power mesh). You need:

1. Matter-capable devices.
2. A **controller** in your home: a smart speaker, display, hub, or similar that supports Matter.
3. For Thread devices, a **Thread border router**, which several speakers and streamers include.

## What it does not do

> **Note:** Matter does not make every feature of every device portable.

- **Not every device type is covered.** The list of supported categories has grown over time but is still shorter than everything on the market. Check the current list for the device type you want.
- **Brand-specific extras stay in the brand app.** Advanced features such as special lighting effects or camera settings may only appear in the maker's own app.
- **It does not replace security hygiene.** Updates, strong account passwords and network separation still matter. See the [security checklist](/learn/secure-your-smart-home-checklist).
- **Older devices do not become Matter.** Some brands offer a firmware update or a bridge for specific products, but many older products will never support it.

## Should you wait for Matter?

No. If a non-Matter device is cheap, well reviewed and solves a real problem, buy it. But when two options are close in price, pick the one with Matter support, because it keeps your options open. Keep your first purchases few and review them after a few weeks, as suggested in [planning your first smart home](/learn/planning-your-first-smart-home).
