A smart device is a small computer that reports on your home. Understanding what it sends helps you decide what you are comfortable with.

## Cloud or local

- **Cloud-dependent devices** send commands through the maker's servers. Your phone app talks to the cloud, which talks to the device. This enables remote access but means the vendor sees usage data and the device may fail if the service is down.
- **Local-control devices** talk directly over your home network. Matter and hub-based systems can work this way, though many still use the cloud for remote access or extra features.

## Typical data by device

| Device | Often collects |
| --- | --- |
| Plug or bulb | On/off times, usage patterns, Wi-Fi name, IP address |
| Energy monitor | Detailed consumption history |
| Camera or doorbell | Video, audio, motion events, snapshots, faces if recognition is on |
| Speaker | Voice recordings, transcripts, history of requests |
| Robot vacuum | Floor maps, schedules, sometimes images |
| Lock | Who unlocked and when |
| Sensors | Occupancy, temperature, when doors open |

Usage patterns alone reveal when you are home and away.

## Read a privacy policy quickly

Look for these in the policy or app settings:

1. **What is collected** and whether it includes audio, images or location.
2. **Why**, and whether data is used for advertising or to train systems.
3. **Who it is shared with** (partners, affiliates, advertisers).
4. **Where it is stored** and for how long.
5. **How to delete it**, and whether the account can be closed.
6. Whether you can **opt out** of analytics and personalisation.

## Reduce what leaves your home

- **Use only the features you need.** Turn off cloud recording, recognition features and voice history if you do not use them.
- **Prefer local options** and Matter when equal in price ([Matter explained](/learn/matter-explained)).
- **Choose the privacy settings** in each app, rather than leaving defaults. Check them after updates.
- **Limit permissions** of the phone app: location, contacts, microphone.
- **Use separate accounts** or an email alias for smart-home devices.
- **Delete old data** regularly and when retiring a device.

## Think before you share

Posting a screenshot of your app can reveal device names, room layout and times you are away. Be careful in public groups.

## Trade-offs are real

Cloud features such as remote access, notifications and voice control are useful. The goal is not zero data, but informed choices.
