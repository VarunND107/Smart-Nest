Cameras and microphones are the devices that can capture the most sensitive information. A few habits keep you in control.

## Know what each device can record

| Device | Can capture |
| --- | --- |
| Indoor camera | Video, often audio, sometimes snapshots on motion |
| Video doorbell | Everyone who approaches, including neighbours and delivery staff |
| Smart speaker/display | Audio after the wake word (and occasionally by mistake); displays may have a camera |
| Robot vacuum with camera | Pictures of rooms and objects |
| Baby monitor | Room video and audio |

Check the specifications and the app's privacy settings for each item. Do not assume.

## Reduce what is captured

- **Disable audio recording** on cameras unless you need it.
- Use **privacy modes** or schedules that switch indoor cameras off when you are home.
- **Cover or unplug** a camera when you do not want it active. Use a physical shutter on displays when available.
- Use the **hardware mute switch** on smart speakers when privacy matters.
- Set **short retention** on stored footage and delete old recordings.
- Turn off **cloud upload** or "improve our service" sharing if you do not want your recordings used that way.

## Respect other people

- **Do not point cameras** at neighbours' homes, shared corridors, bathrooms or bedrooms.
- Inform guests, staff and family where cameras and microphones are, especially indoors.
- Do not publish recordings that show identifiable people without their consent.
- If you share a building, check the society's rules before adding cameras to common areas.

Legal requirements about recording people, particularly audio, vary. If in doubt, take advice and err on the side of notice and consent.

## Protect the footage

- Use unique passwords and 2FA for camera accounts ([accounts guide](/learn/accounts-passwords-and-2fa)).
- **Do not enable public share links.** Treat them as open doors.
- Do not forward camera ports through your router. See [router security](/learn/router-and-network-segmentation).
- Check what the vendor says about **who can access** footage, including employees and third parties.
- Keep firmware up to date ([firmware guide](/learn/firmware-updates-and-end-of-support)).

## When you sell or return a device

A camera or speaker keeps a link to your account until you remove it. Follow [selling or gifting devices](/learn/selling-or-gifting-devices).

## A simple test

Ask: *Would I be comfortable if the people recorded knew exactly what this device captures, where it goes and who can see it?* If not, change the placement or settings.
