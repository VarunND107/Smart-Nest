Firmware is the software inside a device. Updates fix bugs and close security holes. A device that no longer gets updates slowly becomes a liability.

## Why updates matter

- **Security fixes:** weaknesses are discovered over time. Updates patch them.
- **Reliability:** many "random disconnects" are fixed by firmware.
- **New features and compatibility,** such as Matter support on some products.

## How to update safely

1. **Enable automatic updates** in the app where offered, for devices that are not critical.
2. For **locks and hubs**, update manually at a quiet time and when you are home, in case something needs attention.
3. Keep power **stable** during the update. Do not unplug it mid-update.
4. Check that the **battery** is healthy on battery devices before updating.
5. Test the device afterwards: control it, check automations and scenes.
6. Update the **router** and **hub** too, not only the end devices.

> **Note:** If an update seems stuck for more than 30 minutes, power-cycle the device once, wait, and try again. Do not repeat it endlessly. Contact support if it keeps failing.

## Judge support before you buy

Look for answers to these questions on the maker's website or in reviews:

- **How long will it get security updates?** Some vendors state a number of years; many do not.
- **How often have updates been released** for past products?
- Is there a **security contact** for reporting vulnerabilities?
- Does the product work **without the cloud** if the company shuts the service down?

A cheap device that is abandoned after a year costs more than a mid-priced one supported for five.

## When support ends

When a vendor stops updating a device:

1. **Move it to the isolated network**, or remove it from the internet where it can work locally.
2. **Disable remote access and features** you do not need.
3. **Plan to replace it**, starting with cameras, locks and anything exposed to the internet.
4. When you retire it, [remove it from your account and factory reset it](/learn/selling-or-gifting-devices).

## Keep a list

Record each device, its firmware version and the support status. The My home & devices feature in SmartNest is being built so you can track warranty and notes in one place.
