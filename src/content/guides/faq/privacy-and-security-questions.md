## Are smart speakers always listening?

They listen continuously for the **wake word** on the device itself. Audio is meant to be sent to the cloud only after the wake word is detected, but false activations happen. Use the **mute switch** when you want certainty, and review and delete your voice history. See [smart speakers and displays](/learn/smart-speakers-and-displays).

## Who can see my camera footage?

You and anyone you share access with. The vendor may also be able to access footage, depending on its design and policy, particularly if cloud storage is used. Check the privacy policy, use 2FA, and avoid public share links. See [camera and microphone privacy](/learn/camera-and-microphone-privacy).

## Can someone hack my camera?

It is possible, but the usual route is a **stolen password** from another site, not a clever attack on the camera. Use a unique password, 2FA, updated firmware and a secured router. See [accounts, passwords and 2FA](/learn/accounts-passwords-and-2fa).

## Is a guest network worth it?

For cameras, plugs and bulbs, it limits how far a problem can spread. It can break local control and casting for some devices, so test first. See [router and network segmentation](/learn/router-and-network-segmentation).

## What happens if the company shuts down?

Cloud-dependent devices may stop working. Devices with **local control** (and Matter devices) are more likely to keep working. Before buying, check whether the product works without the cloud and what the vendor says about long-term support. See [firmware updates and end of support](/learn/firmware-updates-and-end-of-support).

## Should I use a smart lock on my main door?

It can be safe if you choose a well-reviewed model, keep a **mechanical key**, use 2FA and never automate unlocking. Read [smart locks](/learn/smart-locks).

## Can I trust cheap unbranded devices?

Be cautious. Unbranded devices often lack updates, a clear privacy policy or a security contact. Prefer products from vendors that publish their support terms.

## Do I need to worry about data from plugs and bulbs?

Usage patterns can show when you are home or away. The data is lower risk than camera footage, but still personal. Disable cloud features you do not use. See [what your devices collect](/learn/data-privacy-what-devices-collect).

## How do I check what my devices are doing?

Look at the **list of connected devices** in your router and the **activity logs** in each app. Remove devices you do not recognise.

## What should I do if I think I've been compromised?

Change the account password from a trusted device, turn on 2FA, sign out all sessions, check shared users and automations, and update firmware. See the steps in [accounts, passwords and 2FA](/learn/accounts-passwords-and-2fa).

## Does SmartNest store my device data?

SmartNest stores the details you type in (such as your homes, rooms and notes) in its database so you can see them again. It **does not connect to your devices** and cannot see their live data, video or audio.
