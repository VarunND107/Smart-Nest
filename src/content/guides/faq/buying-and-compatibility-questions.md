## Can I mix brands?

Yes, if they support the same ecosystem or Matter. Choose a primary app or assistant, then check each device's compatibility before buying. See [hubs, bridges and ecosystems](/learn/hubs-bridges-and-ecosystems).

## What does "Works with Alexa/Google/Apple" mean?

It means the product can be controlled through that assistant. It does not tell you how the device connects, whether it works locally, or whether all its features are available through the assistant.

## Do I need Matter devices?

No, but prefer them when the price is close, because they can be shared across ecosystems and work locally. See [what Matter changes](/learn/matter-explained).

## Wi-Fi or Zigbee?

Wi-Fi needs no hub and is easy to start with. Zigbee suits many battery sensors and large setups, and needs a hub. See [how smart devices connect](/learn/how-smart-devices-connect).

## Do I need a hub?

Only if your devices require one. A set of Wi-Fi plugs, bulbs and cameras works without it. See [hubs and gateways](/learn/hubs-and-gateways).

## Why does a device only work on 2.4 GHz?

2.4 GHz has longer range and uses cheaper chips, so most budget devices support only that band. See [connecting a device to Wi-Fi](/learn/connecting-a-device-to-wifi).

## How do I compare two similar devices?

Compare these, in this order:

1. **Compatibility** with your ecosystem.
2. **Connection type** and whether it works locally.
3. **Update and support record.**
4. **Ratings and load limits** (for plugs and switches).
5. **Privacy settings** and storage options (for cameras).
6. **Warranty and return policy.**
7. Price last.

## Is the cheapest option fine?

For a plug or bulb, often yes. For cameras, locks and anything on your front door, spend more on a known vendor with a clear update policy. See [firmware updates and end of support](/learn/firmware-updates-and-end-of-support).

## Should I buy a bundle or starter kit?

Only if it contains devices you would buy anyway. Kits can include things you do not need.

## What about returns?

Check the **return window** before buying and keep the box, receipt and packaging until the device works in its final position. Test pairing and signal at the install spot early.

## How long do smart devices last?

Hardware often lasts years. The limiting factor is **software support**. A device without updates or one that depends on a discontinued app can become useless sooner than the hardware.

## Are the products and prices shown in SmartNest real?

SmartNest shows **sample data** for demonstration. Specifications and prices are illustrative, not verified, and may differ from real products. Always check the maker's page and a retailer before you buy.
