A lock that fits badly jams. Spend the time on measurement and alignment, because that is where most lock problems start.

## Before buying: measure

- **Door thickness** and type: wood, steel, aluminium or glass.
- **Lock type:** deadbolt, mortise, or rim lock, and the **backset** (distance from the door edge to the centre of the lock).
- **Bolt and keyway layout** and whether the existing cylinder is a standard size.
- The inside surface must have enough flat room for the body of a retrofit unit.

If the door opens poorly or the latch drags, **fix the door first**. A smart lock motor cannot overcome a misaligned strike plate for long.

## Fit and align

1. Remove the old parts, keeping fixings and the original key in case you need to revert.
2. Follow the manufacturer's template exactly for holes and spacing.
3. Tighten mounting screws evenly, not hard enough to bend the lock.
4. Check that the **bolt extends and retracts freely by hand** with the door open before connecting power.
5. Close the door and check the bolt enters the strike without rubbing.

## Power and calibration

- Fit fresh batteries of the specified type.
- Run the **calibration** step in the app so the lock learns the locked and unlocked positions.
- Test locking and unlocking at least ten times, with the door open and then closed.

## Enrol users and codes

- Create a separate user for each person; do not share one code.
- Set **temporary access** with start and end times for visitors, cleaners and tradespeople.
- Delete the factory-default or setup codes.

## Test the backup

> **Safety:** Do not leave the house until you have proved the backup works.

1. Unlock with the physical key from outside.
2. Check the emergency power method, if the lock has one.
3. Keep a spare key with someone you trust.

## Secure the account and pairing

- Turn on two-factor authentication for the lock's account ([accounts and 2FA](/learn/accounts-passwords-and-2fa)).
- Do not allow the lock to be unlocked by a voice command without a PIN.
- Keep firmware current ([firmware guide](/learn/firmware-updates-and-end-of-support)).

## Make it reliable

Check alignment seasonally. Wood doors swell in the monsoon and bolts bind. Replace batteries when the low-battery warning appears. For problems, see [lock troubleshooting](/learn/smart-lock-troubleshooting).
