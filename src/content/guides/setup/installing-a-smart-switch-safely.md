> **Safety:** Mains electricity at 230 V can kill. This page explains what the work involves so you can plan it and talk to an electrician. It is **not** a step-by-step instruction for untrained people to wire a switchboard. If you are not trained and competent to do electrical work, hire a licensed electrician.

## Decide who does the work

| Task | Who |
| --- | --- |
| Plug-in smart plugs, screw-in smart bulbs | Anyone |
| Replacing a switch plate, adding relays inside a switch box, wiring a fan module | **Licensed electrician** |
| Anything at the distribution board or MCB panel | **Licensed electrician only** |

If you rent, check your agreement before altering fixed wiring. Landlords often forbid it.

## What an electrician will check

- **Is neutral available in the switch box?** Many Wi-Fi switches need it. See [smart switches and relays](/learn/smart-switches-and-relays).
- **Load per channel** against the switch's rating, with extra headroom for fans and other motors.
- **Earthing** of metal plates and the circuit.
- **Wire condition and size.** Old brittle insulation or undersized wire is a reason to rewire, not to add devices.
- **Box depth and space.** Relay modules need room; crowded boxes overheat.

## The safe working sequence

1. **Isolate.** Switch off the correct circuit breaker (MCB) at the distribution board, label it, and tell everyone in the house.
2. **Prove it is dead.** Test with a proper voltage tester at the switch before touching any wire. Do not trust the wall switch position.
3. **Photograph** the existing wiring and mark each wire.
4. **Connect exactly as the manufacturer's diagram shows**, with correct wire gauge and tight, insulated connections. Loose connections cause heat.
5. **Close and re-energise.** Test the lights, the manual switch and the app before refitting the cover.
6. **Check for heat** after the first hour of use. Warm plates or a smell of burning mean switch off at the MCB immediately.

## Common mistakes

- Using a module on a circuit that exceeds its rating, such as a geyser or heavy motor.
- Putting the wrong module on a fan (see the fan notes in the switches guide).
- Joining wires with tape and not proper connectors.
- Leaving a loose neutral. It can cause intermittent flicker and heat.
- Working with wet hands or on a damp wall.

## After installation

- Label the circuit and keep the wiring diagram with your papers.
- Keep the **manual switch** working. A smart switch should never be the *only* way to turn a light on.
- Record the device in your device list with the install date and electrician details for warranty.

Pair the device afterwards by following [connecting a device to Wi-Fi](/learn/connecting-a-device-to-wifi).
