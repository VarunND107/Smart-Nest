Most installation failures are decided before the device arrives. Run through this list first.

## Network

1. **Wi-Fi reaches the install spot.** Stand there with your phone and test speed and signal. A weak signal in the room means a weak device. See [home network for smart devices](/learn/home-network-for-smart-devices).
2. **Know your Wi-Fi name and password**, and whether your router uses one name for 2.4 and 5 GHz. Many devices need 2.4 GHz only.
3. **Router access.** Know where the admin login is, in case you need to reserve an address or change a setting.

## Power

4. **A socket is available**, or wiring is possible. Cameras and doorbells need a power plan. Battery devices need the right battery type.
5. **Load check.** If the device switches an appliance, compare its rating with the appliance's. Read [smart plugs](/learn/smart-plugs) and [smart switches](/learn/smart-switches-and-relays).
6. **Wiring work is for a licensed electrician.** If it means opening a switch box or touching the distribution board, see [installing a smart switch safely](/learn/installing-a-smart-switch-safely).

## Accounts and apps

7. **Create the vendor account first**, using a strong password and two-factor authentication ([accounts and 2FA](/learn/accounts-passwords-and-2fa)). Use an email address you will keep long term.
8. **Install the right app** from the official store and check it supports your phone's operating system.
9. **Choose your ecosystem** (Google Home, Alexa, Apple Home, SmartThings) and confirm compatibility. See [hubs and ecosystems](/learn/hubs-bridges-and-ecosystems).

## Practical

10. **Keep the box and receipt** until you are sure the device works in place. Check the return window. If you rent, check what you are allowed to change.

## Bring to the install

- Phone, charged, with the app installed
- Wi-Fi details
- Screwdrivers, a spirit level for mounts, and the supplied template
- Spare batteries
- A torch

## During the install

- Do a **dry run** first: pair the device on a table near the router *before* mounting it. A device that works at the router but not in its final spot is a signal problem, not a faulty device.
- Photograph wiring **before** you change anything.
- Change default passwords immediately.

When pairing, go to [connecting a device to Wi-Fi](/learn/connecting-a-device-to-wifi). If something fails, the [troubleshooting flow](/learn/device-offline-troubleshooting-flow) is your next stop.
