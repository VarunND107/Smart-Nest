Lighting is the best place to start. The effects are visible and mistakes are harmless.

## 1. Night-path light

- **Trigger:** motion in the hall or bathroom corridor.
- **Conditions:** after sunset (or between 11 p.m. and 6 a.m.).
- **Action:** turn the light on at **10 to 20% warm white** for 2 minutes, then off.
- **Why:** you can walk safely at night without glaring light.
- **Gotcha:** use a dimmable bulb and make sure motion off does not turn it off while you are still in the room. Add a delay.

## 2. Evening on at sunset

- **Trigger:** 15 minutes before sunset.
- **Conditions:** someone is home.
- **Action:** turn on the porch and living-room lamps.
- **Why:** you stop coming home to a dark house.
- **Gotcha:** check your home location. Sunset times depend on it.

## 3. Leaving home: everything off

- **Trigger:** the last person leaves.
- **Action:** turn off all lights and fans.
- **Why:** saves electricity and removes the "did I leave it on?" worry.
- **Gotcha:** presence detection based on phones needs the right permissions and can be wrong. Also add a manual "Leaving" button.

## 4. Bedtime routine

- **Trigger:** a voice phrase ("good night"), a button, or 10:30 p.m.
- **Action:** turn off living-room lights, dim the bedroom to 20%, turn on a night light, and switch on the fan.
- **Why:** one action instead of a walk through the house.

## 5. Gentle wake-up

- **Trigger:** weekdays at 6:30.
- **Action:** raise bedroom light from 1% to 60% over 10 minutes with a warm tone.
- **Why:** a softer start than an alarm tone.
- **Gotcha:** needs bulbs that support smooth transitions. Cheap bulbs may jump in steps.

## 6. Bathroom or storeroom timer

- **Trigger:** motion detected.
- **Action:** light on, off after 5 minutes with no motion.
- **Why:** saves energy where lights are often left on.
- **Gotcha:** a still person (sitting) may stop triggering the sensor. Use a longer timeout, or a sensor that can detect presence.

## Tips for all of them

- Use **scenes** so you only edit brightness in one place.
- Add a **manual override**: if someone turns a light off by hand, pause the automation for an hour.
- Keep the **wall switch** usable. See [smart switches](/learn/smart-switches-and-relays).
- Do not rely on bulbs if people flip the wall switch ([smart bulbs](/learn/smart-bulbs)).
- Test each rule at the real time of day it will run.

If a rule fails, see [automations not triggering](/learn/automations-not-triggering). For background see [automation basics](/learn/automation-basics) and [smart sensors](/learn/smart-sensors).
